About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at Cardiff University, supervised by Prof. Wei Zhou, and also under the joint guidance of Prof. Paul Rosin and Prof. Hantao Liu. I received my Ph.D. degree from Shandong University of Science and Technology in 2025, supervised by Prof. Qingtian Zeng. My research interests include AIGC-VQA, AIGC-IQA, action recognition, object detection, scene graph generation, multimodal fusion, and related topics in computer vision.
