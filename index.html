About Me

Hi, I am Minghao Zou.

I am currently affiliated with Cardiff University. My research interests include computer vision, multimedia, generative modeling, and quality of experience. I work on visual understanding problems such as procedural task videos, human-object interaction, physics experiment scene parsing, and quality assessment for AI-generated visual media.

Research Focus

  • Visual parsing and representation learning for complex scenes
  • Procedural activity understanding and process mining from video
  • Quality modeling for AI-generated images and videos
